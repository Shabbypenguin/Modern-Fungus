# Fungus

A remake of **Fungus 2.0** (© 1992, 1994 Ryan Koopmans), the classic Mac OS game where fungi grow, trap and eat each other.
It runs in any modern browser, installs as an app (PWA), works offline against CPUs, and plays online with friends.

## In one minute
Each turn you get a random piece. Place it touching your fungus. Trap enemy fungus in a straight line between your own cells and it becomes yours. Cut an enemy off from its head and that part dies. Capture an enemy's **head** and you take their whole fungus. Collect **teeth** to earn **bites**, which chew through enemy fungus without using up your turn. The last fungus alive wins.

## Two ways to play
| | Classic | Modern |
|---|---|---|
| Players | 2–4 | 2–16 |
| Spawns | the original 1994 spots | evenly spaced on a ring |
| Teeth | the four corners | fair spots between neighbours, plus the centre |
| Speeds | 1994 values | New teeth and Bite growth adjustable, with presets |
| Boards | 20 to 100 | 20 to 100 |

Both rule sets work on every board size, from Classic 20×20 to Massive 100×100.

# FAQ

## Playing

**Why can't I place my piece there?**
It has to be fully on the board, on empty cells, and touch your fungus along an **edge**. Corner-to-corner doesn't count. The status line says which rule it broke.

**Why didn't my bite work?**
- Bites must start on an enemy cell right beside your fungus.
- They go in a straight line across or down.
- They can't include heads or your own cells.
- They can't be longer than your bite level allows.
- You need at least one bite.

**Does biting end my turn?**
No. You can bite as many times as you have bites, then place your piece. Bite to open a gap, then fill it.

**My bites just got shorter. Why?**
Bite level follows your current size. If you lose cells and drop below the threshold, your bites shrink again.

**How do I get more bites?**
- Grow onto a tooth.
- Wait for new teeth: everyone gets one every so often.
- Eat a player and inherit all of theirs.

**Why did part of my fungus vanish?**
It was cut off from your head. Every cell must connect back to the head along edges, or it dies.

**What happens if nobody can move?**
After 2 turns per player in a row without anyone placing or biting, the largest fungus wins.

**Is Classic exactly the 1994 game?**
As close as the original code allows. Rules, numbers, start spots and the CPU were all taken from the program itself. New art, sound and controls are the remake's. Anything the original left undefined is marked *(remake)* on [[Rules]].

## The app

**Can I install it?**
Yes. In your browser, use "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen". It then opens in its own window and plays against CPUs offline.

**Does it work on phones?**
Yes: tap to aim, tap again to place, pinch to zoom. Landscape and portrait both work.

**Where are my stats stored?**
In your browser only (**Best fungal growths** on the home screen). Clearing site data resets them. Online games don't count toward them.

**The text is too small or too big.**
Change **Interface size** on the home screen. Board zoom is separate: use the wheel, pinch, or `+`/`−`.

# Controls

Press `?` or **Controls** during a game to see this list in the game.

## Mouse
| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| Left click | Place your piece where it's aimed |
| Right click | Rotate your piece |
| Right-drag or middle-drag | Move around the board |
| Wheel | Zoom in and out where you point |
| Shift + wheel | Rotate your piece |
| Click the piece preview | Rotate your piece |
| In bite mode: press and drag | Bite in a straight line |

The ghost piece under the cursor is tinted when the spot is legal, and marked when it isn't.

## Keyboard
| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `W` `A` `S` `D` | Move around the board |
| `Q` / `E` | Rotate left / right |
| `Space`, arrow keys | Rotate (the 1994 keys) |
| `+` / `−` | Zoom in / out |
| `0` | See the whole board |
| `C` | Centre on your fungus |
| `Enter` | Place at the cursor |
| `B` | Bite mode (`Esc` cancels) |
| `G` | Give up your turn (pass) |
| `P` | Pause (local games only) |
| `?` | Show the controls |

## Touch
| Gesture | Action |
|---|---|
| Tap, then tap again on the same spot | Aim, then place (or tap **Place**) |
| Tap the piece preview | Rotate your piece |
| Drag with one finger | Move around the board |
| Pinch | Zoom in and out |
| In bite mode: press a marked cell and drag | Bite in a straight line |

## Buttons
| Button | Does |
|---|---|
| Rotate | Rotates the piece |
| Place | Places at the aimed spot |
| Bite | Toggles bite mode. Marked cells show where a bite can start. |
| Pass | Ends your turn without moving |
| Whole board | Zooms out to the full board |
| My fungus | Centres on your fungus |
| Pause / Resume | Local games only. Online games run on the server clock. |
| Controls | This list |
| Exit / Leave | Leave the game (asks first). The title bar's close box does the same. |

## Zoom and the minimap
- You can zoom in until about 4×4 cells fill the view, or out to the whole board.
- While zoomed in, a **minimap** appears next to the piece preview. Click or drag on it to jump the view.
- On boards bigger than 35×35 the game starts zoomed in on your fungus. On your turn, the view moves to your fungus if none of it is on screen.

## Online extras
- **Emote buttons** (gg, Nice!, Oops, Whoa, Grr, Yum) appear under the controls. They pop up on your card for everyone.
- There's no pause online. The server runs the turn clock.

## Accessibility
- **Interface size** on the home screen (Small, Normal, Large, Larger) scales all text and buttons. Board zoom is separate.
- Text uses Atkinson Hyperlegible, a font designed for readability.
- Everything works from the keyboard. Status messages are announced to screen readers.
- The game honours your system's **reduced motion** setting.
- Colours are paired with distinct patterns, so players are told apart by more than colour.
